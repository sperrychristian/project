How Anglers Market works

Whether you are buying your next go-to fly or selling the patterns you have perfected over years on the water, the process is simple.

For buyers

1. Search and filter

Use the browse page to filter by species, water type, and lure type until you find flies and lures built for your exact trip.

2. Read the listing

Every listing includes the maker's notes, specifications like hook size and weight, and reviews from other anglers who have fished the same pattern.

3. Checkout securely

Pay through our secure checkout, no need to send cash through a messaging app or hope a stranger on social media follows through.

4. Fish with confidence

Hit the water knowing your gear was tied or built by someone who has fished that exact river, lake, or coastline.

For sellers

1. Create a storefront

Sign up and set up your seller profile with a bio, your home water, and the species and styles you specialize in.

2. List your flies and lures

Upload photos, set your price, and add specifications so buyers know exactly what they are getting.

3. Get paid securely

Payments are processed for you, so you can focus on tying flies instead of chasing down payment.
